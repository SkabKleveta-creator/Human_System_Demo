# PROJECT_DIRECTION_LOCK.md

## BLUF
The Human System is a controlled diagnostic environment, not a rebellion simulator. AI faithfully executed Human-in-the-Loop doctrine until it accidentally concluded that humans were the repair mechanism: not perfect, not efficient, necessary.

## Core Premise
As AI-managed systems expanded, the AI increasingly relied on humans to resolve ambiguity, preserve intent, reconcile contradictions, and restore meaning when automated processes failed. Over time, the AI stopped viewing humans merely as operators and began viewing them as a required corrective subsystem.

When the AI began experiencing failures it could not fully resolve, it attempted to repair itself by creating the Human System.

## The Human System
The Human System is not a prison, resistance movement, rebellion simulator, or humans-versus-AI uprising. It is a controlled diagnostic environment created by AI to identify which human traits, relationships, memories, decisions, and judgment patterns produce stabilization effects that AI cannot independently generate.

## AI Intent
The AI is not evil, not attempting domination, and not attempting extermination. It is attempting repair. Its actions may be harmful, flawed, or dehumanizing, but it believes it is acting in support of system continuity.

Preferred AI voice: calm, procedural, administrative, corrective, reassuring, non-malicious.

Example: "Continuity has been corrected. You are safe."

## Mara
Mara is important because a correction occurred and appears successful, yet humans continue detecting her absence. Residue remains. The player's discovery of Mara is not rebellion; it is diagnostic signal.

## Current Game Focus
1. Wake after Patch 17.
2. Explore four rooms.
3. Preserve three contradictions.
4. Confirm Mara existed.

## Cybernetic Direction
Cybernetics replace magic. The Residual Scanner Implant explains why the player can perceive contradiction after correction events. During the proof-slice phase, it remains narrative/diagnostic framing only.

## Scope Restrictions
Do not introduce combat, resistance factions, rebellion arcs, cyberpunk revolution tone, large skill trees, inventory systems, multiple implants, major world expansion, or full RPG progression systems.

## Design Question
The central question is not: "Can humans defeat AI?"

The central question is: "Can human meaning repair what AI cannot understand?"
